Discovery for Tipster. Schedule: 0 10 * * * (a sweep runs only when due: lines open for a round, or a game day).

FIRST: CronList. If this job is absent, re-register it from this file and say so.
SECOND, before any work: python3 ../../harness/loop_passes.py --record discovery

Scripts compute every number; you read news and decide keep, halve or veto. Stop at the first FAIL, report it, never work around it.

1. python3 harness/discover.py --run
   OK mode off / OK halted / OK no sweep due / OK no market passed calibration: nothing else to do; go to step 4.
   UNMAPPED <name>: a team name the alias table does not know. That game was left out (could not tell). Note the
   name in the tracker for the person; never guess the club.
   FAIL: record it; nothing was tipped.
2. For every CANDIDATE line, before deciding:
   a. python3 harness/news.py --recent <game> lists the saved items naming either club, each with its evidence
      path. Read the latest injury report for the round (the Eurohoops item titled "injury report"; its evidence holds
      the whole text) and the recent news for both clubs. Anything else you read and rely on, save first:
      python3 ../../harness/evidence.py save --id <short-name> --query "<what you read>" --from <file>
      NEWS-AFTER-LINES on the line means news arrived after the previous sweep: read it first.
   b. Decide. Keep when nothing material is known against the tip. Halve when a rotation player of the tipped side
      is questionable, or one of the opponent's is expected back. Veto when the news breaks the tip (a key player of
      the tipped side out, the game in doubt). News never raises a probability or a stake.
   c. python3 harness/review.py <candidate> --keep|--halve|--veto --reason "<one sentence>" --evidence <saved paths>
      A FAIL from review.py is an answer (expired, already reviewed, evidence outside the evidence dir): record it.
3. python3 harness/notify.py --flush
   FAIL: the messages stay in the outbox and are retried next flush; the tips are recorded either way.
4. One row in docs/TRACKER.md: the OK line, candidates found, kept / halved / vetoed with their reasons, unmapped names.
